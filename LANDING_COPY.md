# LANDING_COPY.md — Copy Variants & A/B Test Ideas

## Current Headline (v1)
> Your team is probably overpaying for AI.

**Why this works:** Provocative but not accusatory. "Probably" softens the claim while implying high confidence. Creates immediate relevance for any startup CTO.

---

## Headline Variants to Test

### Variant A — Specificity
> Most startups waste $400/month on redundant AI tools.

**Hypothesis:** A specific dollar figure outperforms a vague claim.

### Variant B — Outcome-focused
> Find out exactly where you're wasting money on AI tools.

**Hypothesis:** "Exactly" appeals to the detail-oriented CTO persona.

### Variant C — FOMO
> Your competitors are auditing their AI spend. Are you?

**Hypothesis:** Social pressure creates urgency. Risk: feels manipulative.

### Variant D — Conversational
> We looked at 50 startup AI stacks. 43 were overpaying.

**Hypothesis:** Social proof via specificity. Requires real data first.

---

## Subheadline (v1)
> StackSave audits your AI tooling subscriptions and identifies exactly where you're leaving money on the table — with specific, actionable recommendations and dollar figures.

**Notes:** Could be shorter. "Leaving money on the table" is a bit cliché.

### Subheadline v2
> Enter your AI tools and monthly spend. In 2 minutes, get a prioritized list of exactly how much you could save — with specific steps to act on it.

## CTA Variants

| Variant | Copy | Hypothesis |
|---|---|---|
| Current | "Run your free audit" | Clear, action-oriented |
| v2 | "Audit my AI spend" | Personalized with "my" |
| v3 | "See my savings →" | Outcome-focused, implies they have savings |
| v4 | "Get instant results" | Speed-focused |

**Best bet:** "Audit my AI spend →" (personal + action + implies outcome)
