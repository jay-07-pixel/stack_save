# GTM.md — Go-to-Market Strategy

## Target Customer

**Primary:** Engineering leads and CTOs at seed to Series A startups (5-50 people) who manage the AI tooling budget.

**Secondary:** Founders and operations leads at bootstrapped companies where the founder directly controls software spend.

**Pain point:** They suspect they're overpaying but don't have time to manually audit 6-10 SaaS subscriptions. They want a 2-minute answer, not a consultancy.

---

## Distribution Channels

### 1. Product Hunt launch (Day 1)
- Post on a Tuesday or Wednesday for maximum exposure
- Tagline: "Find out in 2 minutes if your startup is overpaying for AI tools"
- Ask early adopters in network to upvote
- Offer "launched on PH" badge on homepage

### 2. Twitter / X organic
- Post audit results (anonymized) as social proof
- Thread format: "We audited 50 startups' AI stacks. Here's what we found."
- Tag relevant founders and VCs who post about AI tooling costs

### 3. Hacker News Show HN
- Title: "Show HN: I built a free tool that audits your AI tooling spend"
- Include specific example savings to hook the audience
- Engage aggressively in comments

### 4. Subreddits
- r/startups, r/entrepreneur, r/SaaS
- Share as "I built this after being shocked by our team's AI bills"

### 5. Slack / Discord communities
- Indie Hackers Slack, YC community Discord, Notion startup community
- Post as a tool recommendation, not spam

---

## Growth Loops

**Shareable report → inbound leads**
Every generated audit report has a public URL. When a founder shares their report on Slack or Twitter, it exposes StackSave to their network. The report page has a prominent "Run your own audit" CTA.

**Email capture → newsletter**
Users who enter their email to receive the report are added to a list. A monthly "AI pricing update" newsletter keeps them engaged and drives repeat visits.
